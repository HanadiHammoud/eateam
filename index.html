Errors & Suggested Resolutions
Log issues and fixes · Synced to GitHub
🚨 Log Error / Issue
🚨 Multi-Funnel Follow-up Leakage
Leads from 8+ different sources (VSL, Outbound Genesis, MDP Lead Form, MENA Campaign, Instagram, WhatsApp, Remote Job Funnel, Web Download) are entering the CRM without a unified follow-up protocol. April data: 87 no-shows in Genesis alone with no structured reactivation tracking. MDP leads also sitting unworked.
✅ Resolution
Create a daily priority queue in Zoho filtered by Stage = "No Show" + "Booked > 3 days" + "Needs Follow Up". Assign to Aya for reactivation calls. Maya audits the queue daily. Tag all business-owner Genesis leads for MDP outreach separately.
⚠️ Show-Up Rate Below Target (48.5% vs 60%+ goal)
April: consistent show-up of 40–57% across all weeks, vs the 60–70% target from February planning. Week of Apr 13–19 dropped to 40.5%. At current show rate, this is the single biggest revenue bottleneck — each +5% show rate = ~4 additional closes per month.
✅ Resolution
3-touch confirmation sequence: booking confirm → 24hr reminder → 2hr reminder. Aya calls non-confirmed bookings the day before. Add urgency framing ("your spot is reserved but we need confirmation"). Target: 55%+ by Week 2 May. Maya tracks weekly.
⚠️ Cancellation Rate 57.6% of Raw Bookings
235 of 408 April bookings cancelled before confirmation. This wastes ad spend and setter time. Root cause: low-intent leads booking without commitment, possibly due to low-friction booking flow.
✅ Resolution
Add pre-booking qualifier in setter script (income level, timeline, decision authority). Consider a micro-commitment step (answer 2 questions before booking confirms). Maya tracks cancellation rate weekly — flag if above 50%.
⚠️ No Dedicated MDP Funnel Tracking
MDP leads exist in Zoho with the MDP tag but no dedicated pipeline view showing its own show rates, close rates, revenue. The 15% Genesis-to-MDP business owner crossover is not systematically identified or routed.
✅ Resolution
Create a Zoho CRM view: Tag = MDP, with its own weekly KPI columns. Add a "Business Owner?" qualifier in the Genesis setter script to auto-tag MDP crossover leads. Maya produces a separate MDP report starting Week 1 May.
⚠️ CRM Export: Dates as Excel Serial Numbers
Zoho CRM XLSX export stores Created_Time, Meeting_Date, Closing_Date as floats (e.g. 46143.0) instead of readable dates. Makes date-based analysis and filtering unreliable.
✅ Resolution
Export from Zoho as CSV instead of XLSX — Zoho uses ISO dates in CSV. In Google Sheets, convert with =DATE(1899,12,30)+A2 to convert serial numbers. Maya implements this in the master tracking sheet.
📋 No Setter KPI Accountability System
Aya's daily KPIs (calls, bookings, outbound bookings) have no current logging or accountability beyond informal check-ins. Feb meeting confirmed setter performance is the #1 growth blocker.
✅ Resolution
Use the KPI Logger in Team KPIs tab. Aya fills in daily. Maya verifies against CRM. 2 consecutive days below KPI triggers a notification to Hanadi. Weekly % hitting KPI is Maya's own performance metric.
